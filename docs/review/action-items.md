## House rules for all of them

- **JPG, sRGB, quality ~85, under 300 KB each.** The page already carries about 7 MB.
- **No text anywhere in the picture.** The site runs in English, German and French from
  the same image files, so any word baked into a picture is wrong in two languages out
  of three.
- **No screenshots of the website.** The eight professor portraits currently on the site
  are screenshots of an old version of the card, complete with white margins and the top
  of a caption line baked into the pixels. That is the bug we are replacing.
- **Full bleed, no frame.** The page draws the border, the rounded corners and the
  shadow. Send flat artwork edge to edge, no white margin, no drop shadow.
- **Match the existing illustration style** — the friendly 3D cartoon look of
  `career-ai-doctor.jpg` and the `daily-*.jpg` set.
- **Subject centred.** Several slots crop to fill, so keep faces and the main subject
  away from the edges.
